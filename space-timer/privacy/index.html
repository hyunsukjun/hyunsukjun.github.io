Privacy Policy

For Space Timer Host, Space Timer, and support inquiries.

OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026.

Who operates the apps

Space Timer Host for Mac and Space Timer for iPhone and iPad are operated by Hyunsuk Jun. This policy covers both apps and support inquiries sent to hyunsukjun2025@gmail.com.

Information exchanged between your devices

The apps use local network access to discover and connect the Mac host and performer devices. Connected devices exchange the score, time signatures and tempos, playback and stopwatch state, and display text entered on the Mac. They also use connection identifiers, join authorization information, player numbers, network addressing information, and timing information to establish connections, reconnect, and synchronize playback.

No online service account is required. The apps do not include advertising or usage analytics services, and they do not send your performance information to a server operated by the developer.

Information stored on your devices

Mac
Saved presets contain the preset name, score changes, and ending measure. Changes to an active saved preset may also be saved when the host window closes or the app quits. The host also stores the editor size, language preference, join code, and authorization information used to reconnect performer devices.
iPhone and iPad
Authorization information for reconnecting to the last approved host is stored in the device’s Keychain. Received scores and playback state are processed in memory while the app runs.

Saved presets remain on the Mac until you delete them. Preferences and saved connection information may persist across app launches. These local app records are separate from support email and are not subject to the 90-day support retention period.

Delete presets and revoke access

  • Delete saved presets using the trash button in Space Timer Host.
  • Remove a Player in the Mac host’s connection list to revoke that device’s authorization. Regenerate the join code to require all previously authorized devices to join again.
  • Turn off Local Network access in your device settings to withdraw that permission. Connecting the apps requires this access.

Revoking authorization prevents that authorization from being used to reconnect; it does not mean every stored record has been erased from the other device. Contact support if you need help with locally stored information.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Support inquiries

If you email support, we receive your email address, any name included in your message, your inquiry, and any attachments or device and app details you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and troubleshoot the issue.

Support messages are handled through the Gmail address provided in this policy. Your email provider and Gmail process the messages as part of delivering and storing email. This is separate from the apps’ device-to-device communication.

We delete support correspondence and associated attachments, including copies retained for troubleshooting, 90 days after the inquiry is closed. This retention period applies to support information held by the operator, not to copies in your own mailbox or to information stored locally by the apps.

Privacy questions and deletion requests

For privacy questions or to request deletion of support information before the 90-day period ends, email hyunsukjun2025@gmail.com. Please identify the inquiry concerned, preferably by replying to the original email thread. Support is provided in English.
