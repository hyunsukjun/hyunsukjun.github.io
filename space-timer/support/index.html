Space Timer Support

Setup, playback, and troubleshooting for Space Timer Host and Space Timer.

OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

Connect your devices

  1. Open both apps.Open Space Timer Host on your Mac and Space Timer on your iPhone or iPad. For the initial connection, use the same Wi-Fi network and keep both apps open.
  2. Allow Local Network access and select your Mac.If prompted, allow Local Network access. Select your Mac from the hosts shown in the mobile app.
  3. Enter the four-digit join code.Enter the code displayed by the Mac host, then join. The mobile device can reconnect without entering the code again while its authorization remains valid.

Run a rehearsal or performance

Score

On the Mac, enter the time signature and tempo changes for your measures. Choose a starting measure and press Play. The performer display shows the count-in, followed by the current measure and beat. Use Stop on the Mac to stop playback.

Stopwatch

Set a starting time in minutes and seconds, then press Play. After the count-in, connected performer displays show the running time from your chosen starting value.

Text you type in Display Text on the Mac appears immediately on connected performer screens. You can edit it while Stopwatch is running. In Score mode, stop playback before editing.

Troubleshooting

My Mac does not appear on the mobile device.

Check that both apps are open and Local Network access is allowed in your device settings. Try the same Wi-Fi network. Some public or guest networks block communication between devices; try a network that permits device-to-device connections.

I am asked to enter a join code again.

If the host regenerated the code or removed your Player authorization, enter the current code shown on the Mac.

The connection was interrupted during playback.

In Score mode, the performer display can continue if the device already received the score and start information, had synchronized its timing with the Mac, and keeps Space Timer on screen. Changes on the Mac reach that device when it reconnects and receives the host’s current state. Check the connection before resuming coordinated playback.

When are changes to a saved preset stored?

Changes to an active saved preset may also be saved when the host window closes or the app quits. Review the preset before closing if you want to keep the earlier version.

How do I remove a preset or revoke a device’s access?

Use the trash button on the Mac host to delete a saved preset. Remove a Player from the host’s connection list to revoke that device’s authorization. Regenerating the join code requires previously authorized devices to join again.

Contact support

On iPhone or iPad, tap the circled info icon (ⓘ) at the top right of the Mac selection screen. Once connected, it appears to the right of the Player label when playback is stopped. The icon is hidden during playback. In Info, choose Support Page, tap the email address, or open Privacy Policy.

On Mac, open the settings gear and choose Contact Support or Privacy Policy.
